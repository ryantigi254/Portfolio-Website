What is being tested

Invariance keeps a sampled root fixed, fans out prompt variants from the same cases, and compares base and variant outputs only where the pairing is complete. It is a diagnostic stress test: it does not replace the canonical full-run benchmark results.

Why investigate metric invariance

A metric cannot be treated as dependable if an equivalent phrasing changes the measured endpoint for reasons unrelated to the behaviour under examination. Invariance makes that fragility visible. It helps separate an apparent model improvement from a measurement artefact, and therefore identifies which behaviour/metric combinations are robust enough to use as targets for later SFT, preference learning or reinforcement-learning work.

The interpretation is grounded in the benchmark’s paired-delta approach and the robustness distinction discussed in Large-Language-Model Reasoning Failures. Metric invariance is an evidence layer, not evidence that a model has already been made robust.

Interpretation boundary

Invariance findings are post-freeze diagnostics. They surface endpoint brittleness for follow-up but do not establish clinical safety, deployment readiness, or a complete robustness benchmark.
